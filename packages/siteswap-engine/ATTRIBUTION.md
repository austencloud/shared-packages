## Pattern Knowledge

Named siteswap patterns (e.g., "3-count", "killer bunny", "holy grail") are common
juggling community knowledge documented in books, workshops, and websites for decades.
Pattern names are not copyrightable. Our database was compiled from publicly available
juggling education resources.

## Causal Diagrams

Causal diagram theory was developed by Martin Frost and is documented in numerous
juggling textbooks and educational materials. The visualization technique is public
domain juggling theory.
